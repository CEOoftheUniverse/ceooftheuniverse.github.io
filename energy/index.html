One-line consensus (6/6). The only real household-scale business is (D) soil/fertiliser via an electric composter — but as a kitchen-waste convenience + hardware-&-consumables business, not a fertiliser-sales or energy business. Biogas (A), electricity (B) and whole-house heating (C) all fail at single-household scale in Poland on physics, winter and economics. The draft's direction is right; its winter number, device pick and "no PL retailer" claim need correcting.

The four end-products — quantified

🪴 Soil / fertiliser THE business

Indoor electric composter → 0.15–0.4 kg amendment/day, year-round, no gas permits. Output is pre-compost (needs 2–6 wk curing); the money is convenience + filters/microbes, not fertiliser sales. Grade A.

🍳 Cooking gas warm-season hobby

~0.1–1 m³ biogas/day ≈ 15–30 min cooking. Dies <15–20 °C; winter output drops 90–100%, and heating the digester costs more energy than the gas it makes. Grade A.

⚡ Electricity not a product

0.05–0.3 kWh/day vs 10–30 kWh/day home use. The draft's "up to 2 kWh/day" needs ~10–12 kg waste/day — a restaurant. Grade A.

🔥 Whole-house heat physically dead

2–4 kWh thermal/day vs 40–100 kWh/day needed in a Polish January — <1.5% of demand. Grade A.

🇵🇱 Poland cold-climate reality

  • Winter drop is worse than the draft: below 15 °C output falls 90–100% (not 50–70%); at 0 °C the slurry freezes and splits PVC bladders.
  • Heating the digester is a net-energy LOSER: holding 35 °C costs 2–6 kWh/day > the ~0.6–1.8 kWh/day of gas produced.
  • Burial to the frost line (0.8–1.2 m) holds ~6–8 °C — protects from frost, does not create the warmth yield needs.
  • Permitting (dissent): the <50 kW exemption is agricultural only; urban household food-waste digestion likely falls under the Waste Act (grey area). Grok dissents (own-use gas = no formalities). → get a PL legal opinion.
  • PL retailer EXISTS (corrects the draft): Leroy Merlin PL sells an S&G home biogas unit ~6,999 zł; Reencle on Allegro 1,998 zł; Geolia garden composters ~189–255 zł.
  • Missed incentive: municipal composting rebate (ulga za kompostownik) ~5–9 zł/person/month — but targets garden composters; indoor electric is gmina-dependent.

🛒 Sourcing + ship-to-Poland

  • Alibaba Turtle $290–310 FOB / PUXIN $183–298 FOB → €650–800 landed (LCL Gdańsk + 23% VAT + duty + damage, no EU CE).
  • AliExpress EU-warehouse 3 L dehydrators €155–220 VAT-paid; warranty ~nil.
  • JD.com rarely ships PL direct; forwarder + plug/CE mismatch.
  • PL/EU retail Reencle Prime 1,998 zł (~€460) with 2-yr rękojmia; best total cost once VAT + spares counted.
  • Safety/legal: biogas burners fall under EU 2016/426 (GAR) — Polish customs (KAS) seizes uncertified gas gear and it voids home fire insurance. New EU small-parcel: €3 duty (1 Jul 2026) + €2/item (1 Nov 2026).
🚗 Gas-for-car — unanimous NO. One home's scraps ≈ 0.15–0.5 kg CH₄/day ≈ 1.5 km/day (10–18 km/month). Upgrading + compressing to 200–250 bar costs 0.4–0.8 kWh/Nm³ and €8,000–25,000+ in gear; ≥4 L @ 200 bar triggers mandatory UDT pressure-vessel registration. Hydrogen is worse (~55 kWh/kg H₂, 700 bar). EV + rooftop PV wins by 1–2 orders of magnitude (~€2.50/100 km on a night tariff). Keep any biogas for cooking.
💶 Economics the draft lacked. Reencle Prime ≈ 1,998 zł + 456 kWh/yr power (~456 zł) + 279 zł/yr filter ≈ ~1,135 zł/yr ≈ 8.2 zł per kg of waste. It is a paid convenience, not a profit centre — a passive vermicomposter or Bokashi beats it on money. Output-quality flag: electric-composter output can be immature / phytotoxic ("soil-like" ≠ mature compost ≠ legally sellable fertiliser); Reencle (microbial) still needs 2–6 wk curing, Lomi only dehydrates (BioCycle; Voběrková 2026; American University study).

🔀 Dissents / consensus stress-test

  • PL biogas retailer? YES (4 seats, Leroy Merlin S&G) vs NO (Qwen, DeepSeek) → resolved: exists, doesn't fix winter.
  • Permitting? Waste-Act grey area (5 seats) vs "no formalities for own-use gas" (Grok) → open legal question.
  • Sell the compost? Regulated (EU 2019/1009; Gemini adds MRiRW + EU 1069/2009) → don't assume resale revenue.
  • Best device? Gravity (Qwen/Grok) vs Prime (draft) vs Urbalive/Bokashi (GPT-5/DeepSeek, best economics) vs Oklin (Gemini, B2B) → segment by customer.
