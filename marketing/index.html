🌱 Worked example - Venture #4: SEO Content Engine full run

🔍 Intelligence -> intel.json

Search is moving to AI answers (AEO/GEO) - keyword-stuffed content is losing; "content AI engines cite" is the wedge. Competitors: cheap AI mills (low trust), boutique agencies (slow, pricey), DIY tools. Gap = managed, mid-price, citation-engineered SEO content. ICP pains: "I rank but get no traffic", "AI Overviews stole my clicks", "I can't stay consistent."

🧩 Strategy -> strategy.md

Positioning: "SEO content for the AI-answer era - written to be cited, not just ranked." Channels (by fit): LinkedIn thought-leadership -> X teardown threads -> YouTube/TikTok how-to -> a lead magnet ("AEO Checklist") -> small TikTok Ads test. Budget of $500/mo: 60% organic + magnet, 25% TikTok Ads, 15% tools. KPIs: 30d = 25 magnet downloads; 60d = 3 calls; 90d = 2 retainers (~$1-3k MRR).

✨ Content-Production -> content_pack/

LinkedIn post

"Google's AI Overview just answered your customer - using someone else's page. Here's the 4-part structure that gets yours cited instead." (carousel)

X thread hook

"I audited 10 SaaS blogs. 9 are invisible to AI answers. The 1 that isn't does these 5 things:"

TikTok / Short (30s)

Hook "ranking #1 and still no traffic?" -> show AI Overview stealing the click -> the fix -> CTA to the free checklist.

Posting schedule (Postiz)

Mon LI - Tue X - Wed TikTok how-to - Thu LI carousel - Fri YT short - Sat repost to Threads/Reddit - Sun magnet promo.

channel_setup.md: connect LI/X/TikTok/YouTube/Threads in Postiz; TikTok Ads = 1 campaign, 3 hook variants, $10/day, optimize for checklist landing-page views.

📦 Worked example - Venture #1: Junk Brokerage compact run

Taken as an asset-light junk-removal / clearance brokerage - connect people needing junk hauled with vetted local haulers, own the demand + booking, broker jobs for a margin, no trucks owned. Swap freely if the ledger scopes it differently - the leg is venture-agnostic.

🔍 Intelligence

Demand is hyperlocal + visual; incumbents (1-800-GOT-JUNK, LoadUp) win on trust/speed. Gap = instant upfront price + satisfying before/after + asset-light margin. Hooks: oddly-satisfying before/after Reels, hoarder clear-outs, price-reveal.

🧩 Strategy

"Book junk removal in 60 seconds - vetted local haulers, upfront price." Channels: TikTok Ads + organic before/after Reels -> Google Local/LSAs -> Facebook community -> 60-sec booking page. Budget $300/mo: 55% TikTok Ads, 25% Google Local, 20% page/tools. KPI: cost/lead < $8, booking > 20%, margin/job 25-40%.

✨ Content

TikTok before/after script + 5 hooks; "upfront price, gone today" offer copy; 7-day Postiz schedule of before/after Reels cross-posted to IG/FB/Shorts; TikTok Ads brief (3 before/after variants, $10/day, geo = one metro, optimize booking clicks).

Both ventures used the identical leg - only venture_profile.json changed. That is the whole point. 💕

▶️ How to run it

  1. Trigger /market <venture_id> (add to OMNISPHERE-TRIGGERS.md) expands to "run the marketing leg on this profile."
  2. Fast pass: the Orchestrator runs the 3 agents on the newest models -> plan + content + setup in one turn.
  3. Consensus upgrade (optional): send the Strategy step to the full Omnisphere panel (P1-P20, @grok-on-X fallback) via the consensus queue; until it runs, per-AI columns read pending panel.
  4. Dispatch to the VM: queue the brief to Drive CURATOR-INBOX.md. Queue is not delivery - reported "queued, not yet picked up" until the VM loop visibly pulls it.
  5. You stay in control: nothing posts, pays, or touches keys without your one-click go.

Cost (price-normalized)

Build ~$0 vs $15,000 for the Structure system (payback: immediate). Per-venture run ~$2-8 in tokens on metered APIs, or ~$0 on your flat-rate GLM-5.3 / OAuth plans. Postiz ~$0-29/mo; ad spend your choice (start a $150-300/mo TikTok Ads test per venture).
